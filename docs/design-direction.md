# Workspace design

Palette: mist #eef3f6, ink #183347, table blue #245e78, line #c4d1d9,
geometry orange #a74317, white #ffffff.
Typography: local Avenir Next or Segoe UI; clear sentence-case headings.
Layout: left-aligned introduction, wide video on the left, narrower preparation
controls on the right; stack on small screens.

```text
Sports Replay Analyst                         Local video workspace
Start with the table.
[                   video                   ][ preparation ]
```

The memorable element is the table-blue video surface. Avoid decorative metric
cards, fabricated trajectories, external assets, and a marketing hero. The initial
plan was reviewed against the brief: the video and its preparation take priority;
statistics and nonfunctional analysis buttons are omitted. Geometry uses orange
with text labels so meaning does not depend on color alone.
